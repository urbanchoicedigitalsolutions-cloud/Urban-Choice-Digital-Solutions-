05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about our services and how to get started.

What is Urban Choice?

Urban Choice is a digital solutions brand helping individuals, businesses and organisations bring ideas to life through AI-powered technology, automation and creative digital services.

What services do you offer?

We offer AI chatbot and agent development, business automation, website design and development, AI image creation, promotional videos and other customised digital solutions.

Can you create a chatbot for my business?

Yes. We can discuss your business needs and create a chatbot configured with relevant information to answer common customer questions and guide conversations.

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot primarily communicates with users. An AI agent can also be designed to perform specific tasks and support workflows, depending on its tools and configuration.

Can you automate parts of my business?

Yes. Tell us about the repetitive tasks or workflow challenges you want to improve. We'll discuss suitable automation options.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Website projects are designed to be responsive across mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can I request changes to a design or website?

Yes. Requirements and the number or scope of revisions should be agreed before the project begins.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the service, project complexity and requirements. Contact us with your brief for a quotation.

How long does a project take?

Delivery time depends on the scope and complexity. We'll provide an estimated timeline after discussing your requirements.

How do I get started?

Send us an enquiry using the contact form or WhatsApp. We'll discuss your idea, requirements and next steps.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Post-delivery support and maintenance arrangements depend on the project and the agreement reached before delivery.
